About

Hi! I’m Alisya, a final-year MEng Mechanical Engineering with Nuclear Engineering student at Imperial College London. My final-year project focuses on the thermomechanical analysis of hypersonic propulsion systems.

I’m passionate about taking ideas from initial concepts through design and analysis to building and testing them in real life. Through the different projects I’ve taken on, I’ve modelled engineering systems across a range of disciplines in Python, Simulink, MATLAB, Ansys and Abaqus.

I particularly enjoy problems that span disciplines, as they give me the chance to explore unfamiliar areas and develop practical solutions to real-world challenges.
